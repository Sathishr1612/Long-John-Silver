Our Story

Where Every Meal
Feels Like Home

Nestled near Polo Towers in the heart of Shillong, Long John Silver's has been a beloved culinary haven since 2020. We envisioned a cozy cafe that would not only serve delicious food but also become a cherished gathering spot for our community.

Our menu is a thoughtful curation of Continental, Chinese, and Indian cuisine — bringing together familiar comfort foods and exciting global tastes. Each dish is prepared with passion, using fresh, quality ingredients for a soul-satisfying experience every time.

We're proud to offer a space that feels instantly welcoming — designed for you to unwind and savor every bite, whether it's a quick lunch, leisurely dinner, or a special occasion.
